New Zealand timber art, gifts & décor for retail stores.

Designed to last. Made to be noticed.

furnACE creates handcrafted timber décor and giftware inspired by Aotearoa’s native birds, landscapes, rural materials and Kiwiana character.

Each product is designed in-house, made in small batches, and finished by hand using woodworking, laser cutting, engraving, staining, spraying and careful assembly.
